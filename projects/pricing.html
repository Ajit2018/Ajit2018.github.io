Business problem

Discounting can increase conversion while destroying contribution economics.

The management decision is not “did bookings rise?” It is “where does incremental demand justify the price / margin cost?”

01

Conversion trap

A higher booking rate can be purchased with unnecessary discount.

02

Segment averages

Portfolio averages can conceal where the treatment helps or hurts.

03

Incrementality

Gross response does not prove that the treatment created new demand.

04

Economic objective

Pricing must optimise value, not a single funnel KPI.

Commercial guardrail

Evaluate demand, contribution, incrementality, cannibalisation and constraints together before expanding a treatment.

Methods

EXP

Experiment readout

Contribution economics

Booking response is translated into margin implications rather than treated as the objective.

SCEN

Alternative response assumptions expose decision sensitivity and reduce false precision.

MEMO

Responsible data & AI

Transparent synthetic treatment; deterministic business calculations.

Data boundary

Public hotel-booking observations support the demonstration. The pricing intervention is explicitly synthetic.

AI boundary

Deterministic calculations produce the experiment and economic facts; AI may summarise them but is not the source of truth.

Claim boundary

No proprietary hotel / client data, no production deployment and no client performance claim.
