PRODUCT

Decision product thinking

Start from the user, decision and operating workflow — then decide where AI actually adds value.

GOVERN

Quantitative truth first

Numbers come from governed data, rules and models. AI retrieves and explains; it should not invent the economics.

ADOPT

Practical transformation

Automation matters only when people trust the outputs, understand the boundaries and can integrate them into real work.

Business problem

Many AI initiatives start with the model. Useful transformation starts with the decision and workflow.

The central question is not “where can we add a chatbot?” but “which work should remain deterministic, which reasoning can AI accelerate, and where must a human stay accountable?”

01

Unclear value

AI demos can look impressive without changing a decision, process or economic outcome.

02

Hallucinated facts

Generative systems should not become the authority for financial, operational or risk numbers.

03

Fragmented workflow

People lose time moving between data, documents, dashboards and repeated manual steps.

04

Adoption / accountability

Teams need transparency, permissions, review points and clear ownership before consequential use.

Operating architecture

Separate quantitative truth from AI reasoning.

1Business problem & user

Define the decision, cadence, constraints and accountable human.

2Data / deterministic memory

Facts, metrics, calculations, permissions, risk limits and workflow state.

3Analytical tools

Python, SQL, forecasting, optimisation, rules, experiments or domain models.

4AI reasoning layer

RAG, tool calling, summarisation, hypothesis generation and narrative.

5Human-in-the-loop action

Review, override, approve and learn from outcomes.

Design principle:

AI can be creative where creativity helps; it should be constrained where facts, money, safety or customer consequences require exactness.

Methods

A pragmatic toolkit around the workflow.

LLM

Reasoning & narrative

Use language models for synthesis, structured reasoning, drafting and explanation where probabilistic output is acceptable.

RAG

Grounded retrieval

Bring governed documents and evidence into context rather than relying on model memory.

TOOLS

Tool calling

Let AI invoke deterministic calculations, searches or workflows instead of reproducing them unreliably in prose.

AGENT

Agentic workflow

Decompose longer work into observable steps with state, checkpoints and explicit permissions.

DATA

Python / SQL / BI backbone

Preserve structured data, reproducible calculations and management outputs as the factual layer.

HITL

Human review

Use confidence / consequence gating so the human stays in control when decisions matter.

Responsible AI

Governance is an architecture choice, not a disclaimer at the end.

Quantitative authority

Deterministic systems, governed data and validated models calculate the facts.

AI authority

AI retrieves, reasons, explains and orchestrates within defined permissions; it does not silently change hard risk or financial truth.

Human authority

People remain accountable for consequential actions and can inspect evidence, assumptions and uncertainty.

Claim boundary:

This page describes current practice, POCs and design philosophy. It does not claim enterprise-scale production MLOps or autonomous AI deployment.
