Business problem

Engineering decisions must satisfy physics, consumer needs, manufacturability, cost and time at the same time.

A technically elegant design can still fail if it cannot be manufactured robustly, misses consumer value or creates unacceptable cost and quality risk.

01

Performance

Does the concept work under real thermal, structural, fluid and usage conditions?

02

Robustness

Can it survive variation, drops, heat cycles, multiphase behaviour and manufacturing tolerances?

03

Industrialisation

Can suppliers and factories build it repeatedly at the required scale and quality?

04

Economics

Does the technical solution support cost, timeline and product-value targets?
