Business question

Which corporate accounts need attention now, why, and what evidence should the relationship manager see before acting?

Solution

A governed evidence stack spanning transactions, cash outlook, liquidity context, behavioural groups and explainable change signals.

Major finding

Complexity alone did not solve monitoring: transparent rules over-alerted, while Isolation Forest missed too much. The hybrid remains contextual evidence only.

Evidence boundary: Real ABN AMRO sandbox data is used for API and schema fidelity; synthetic longitudinal data is used for modelling and controlled evaluation. Independent portfolio POC — not an ABN AMRO production deployment.
Relationship-manager problem

Traditional reporting shows what happened. The decision requires context about what may happen next.

The design focuses on the evidence a manager needs before initiating a customer conversation—not an automatic customer decision.

Why transaction intelligence?

  • Balances alone hide cash-flow timing and counterparty dependence.
  • Portfolio thresholds ignore each account's own operating rhythm.
  • A low balance can be persistent, temporary or newly emerging.
  • An alert without evidence creates workload, not intelligence.

Decisions to support

  • Which accounts deserve review this week?
  • Is pressure new, persistent or forecast?
  • Which flows, counterparties or recurring patterns changed?
  • What should the manager verify before acting?
Management design principle

Surface the account, reason codes, evidence and uncertainty. Keep the human relationship manager accountable for interpretation and action.

Forecasting

Absolute balance targets proved poorly posed for relationship risk. Drawdown and new-entry liquidity targets offered clearer interpretation, with simple baselines retained where preferable.

Clustering

K-means with six clusters provided interpretable context. Stability was moderate, so clusters are descriptive—not permanent customer types.

Change detection

Rules found more events but overwhelmed users; Isolation Forest controlled workload but missed too much. The hybrid is a supporting severity signal, not a decision.

Technical deep dive

Reproducible data engineering, causal features and post-freeze validation.

Technical depth is organized around controls and evidence—not a model-development log.

API and canonical schema

Read-only account, balance and paginated activity capture. Versioned schemas preserve source IDs, arrays, raw JSON and deterministic SHA-256 activity keys.

Bronze / Silver pipeline

PySpark normalizes immutable raw JSON, parses proper dates and decimals, retains type-specific fields and saves canonical Parquet tables with automated quality reports.

SQL analytics

Explicit reusable queries cover account summaries, flow direction, running balances, concentration, frequency, mutation codes and quality diagnostics.

Causal forecasting

Chronological within-account splits, lagged and rolling features, no future leakage, mandatory mean/median/seasonal baselines and post-hoc event/archetype slices.

Behavioural clustering

Account-level common window, skew transformations, correlation pruning, robust scaling, K-means/GMM comparison and seed/bootstrap/window stability.

Change detection

Account-day median/MAD rules, percentage change, EWMA shifts, account-relative thresholds and one lightweight Isolation Forest trained only on observable history.

Leakage control

Synthetic archetypes and 380 injected-event labels were hidden during features, thresholds, model selection and tuning. They were joined only after decisions were frozen.

Responsible AI

Clear evidence boundaries and human accountability.

This case study is intentionally explicit about what the work does—and does not—demonstrate.

What is real

  • ABN sandbox endpoints and response schemas
  • Read-only API integration and pagination
  • Bronze/Silver/SQL pipeline implementation
  • Reproducible analytical methodology

What is synthetic

  • Longitudinal 400-account history
  • Behavioural archetypes and injected events
  • Model performance and operational workload evidence
  • Ground truth used only after detector freeze

Deterministic analytics versus AI

Data pipelines and quantitative models remain the authority for calculations. Planned AI retrieves and explains governed outputs; it does not invent the numbers.

Claims not made

No autonomous customer decision, no AML or fraud model, no production ABN deployment, and no validated production banking performance.

Human in the loop

A relationship manager should see evidence, assumptions, reason codes and uncertainty before deciding whether and how to engage a customer.
