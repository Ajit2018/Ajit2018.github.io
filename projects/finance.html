Business problem

Global pricing creates value only when policy, market execution and management visibility stay aligned.

Across international markets, pricing differences can reflect legitimate strategy, local conditions, timing or execution — but they can also create arbitrage and value leakage. Senior leaders need a governed way to see the signal, understand the cause and decide what action is appropriate.

01

Fragmented visibility

Global signals can be obscured when market, product and pricing data are reviewed through different definitions or local views.

02

Signal ≠ diagnosis

A price difference is not automatically a problem. Management needs context and drill-down before intervention.

03

Cross-functional ownership

Pricing outcomes span Commercial Policy, Pricing, Finance, Supply, R&D and markets; no single dashboard can substitute for coordinated action.

04

Executive-to-root-cause gap

Leadership needs a concise signal while analysts and market teams need enough detail to investigate the underlying drivers.

The management questions Where are the material discrepancies?• What explains them?• Which market / product / policy needs attention?• Who should act?

Governance & claim discipline

Trusted management analytics starts with governed quantitative truth.

This professional case study predates the current GenAI wave; its relevance to AI is the operating discipline: governed metrics and reproducible quantitative outputs should remain authoritative, while AI can later assist retrieval, explanation and workflow.

Authoritative numbers

Quantitative calculations and KPI definitions belong in governed data / analytics layers, not generated narrative.

Access & confidentiality

The original solution used secure live dashboards. This public case study intentionally excludes confidential Philips data and screenshots.

Human accountability

Pricing and finance analytics support decisions; they do not replace market, policy or leadership judgement.

Claims not made

No statutory-accounting ownership, no formal FP&A leadership claim, no production GenAI deployment, and no causal attribution of all identified value to the analytics product.
