Business problem

Commercial value can leak through many small execution differences — unless the organisation can see, diagnose and act on them consistently.

The work spanned pricing undercuts, harmonisation exposure, price erosion, trade investment, tax documentation and business-case monitoring. The common need was governed evidence that could move from management signal to root cause.

01

Pricing leakage

Transaction-level prices can deviate from target / policy and accumulate material exposure across markets, customers and SKUs.

02

Price erosion

Managers need to distinguish where realised price is deteriorating and which products are more or less price elastic.

03

Trade-investment granularity

Large STTI pools need classification into regular promotions, phase-out and unhealthy-stock related investment.

04

Risk documentation

Group Tax needed centralised evidence and cost-centre documentation to support tax-authority discussions and reduce non-deductibility risk.

Decision system

Reusable analytics products rather than one-off analysis.

The strongest evidence shows a recurring pattern: integrate data, define decision logic, operationalise the analysis, validate with stakeholders and create a repeatable management process.
